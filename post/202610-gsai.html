An Update on Joining Our Research Group

Until now, prospective students could join our research group through one of two programs at Seoul National University (SNU): the Department of Intelligence and Information (DoII) at the Graduate School of Convergence Science and Technology (GSCST), or the Interdisciplinary Program in Artificial Intelligence (IPAI) at the College of Engineering.

Starting in 2027, these two routes will become a single path through the Graduate School of Artificial Intelligence (GSAI). SNU will bring together DoII, IPAI, and the Graduate School of Data Science under GSAI.

We’re excited about the possibilities this change will bring, and we’ll share more as they unfold. For now, the key takeaway is simple: if you’d like to join our research group, please apply to GSAI at SNU.
